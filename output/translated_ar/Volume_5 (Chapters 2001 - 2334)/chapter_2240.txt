To conclude —

The five regions and two heavens had a vast number of refinement path dao marks, but they were not evenly distributed, some places were dense while others were sparse. These refinement path dao marks were very stable and hard to change, normally speaking, their growth was extremely slow and needed a lot of time to accumulate.

These refinement path dao marks could be seen as the basis of the entire world's refinement path foundation.

On this foundation, a large number of beasts, plants, and lifeless غو materials were produced and nurtured.

These beasts and plants were related to refinement path, they were refinement path resources.

Normally speaking, in places with dense refinement path dao marks, the relevant resources would also be more abundant and high quality. In places with sparse refinement path dao marks, there would be fewer refinement path resources and of lower quality.

The refinement path dao marks and resources had a deep influence on each other. For example, the coral group that فانغ يوان saw earlier was a refinement path resource point, the source was an immemorial sunken fish that died thousands of years ago, its refinement path dao marks scattered in the surroundings and a portion was left behind in that location.

The dao marks that فانغ يوان refined here were the refinement path foundation of the world.

Refinement path resources were not included in this.

The natural dao marks that فانغ يوان refined were like a huge spider web. As long as فانغ يوان was in range, he would be able to sense all refinement path activities within the web.

This was the so-called refinement path sensation.

فانغ يوان had a vague sensation of the refinement path resources, he could also sense the refinement path activities of غو cultivators.

In places with dense refinement path dao marks, the sensation would be more intense, stable, and clearer. For example, he would be able to find small-scale ordinary refinement path غو materials.

Conversely, if the refinement path dao marks were sparse, the refinement path sensation would be weaker. In those regions, فانغ يوان would only be able to sense refinement path immortal materials.

باستخدام علامات داو لمسار التحسين التي قام بصقلها، يمكن لفانغ يوان التأثير على كل شيء في هذا النطاق، ويمكنه التدخل في الكائنات الحية وكذلك البيئة الطبيعية.

إن مجرد استخدام علامات داو لمسار التحسين لتضخيم قوته كان مجرد الاستخدام الأساسي.

"من زاوية مسار الصقل، في عالم زراعة الغو بأكمله، تعد المناطق الخمس والسماوات مجرد مادة خالدة عملاقة. هذه المادة الخالدة تشمل كل شيء، ولها علامات داو لجميع المسارات."

"على الرغم من أنني أصبحت موقرًا في مسار الصقل، إلا أنه لا يمكنني سوى تحسين جزء من هذه المادة الخالدة الضخمة. وهذا ينطبق على موقر الشمس العملاقة الخالد، وموقر كوكبة النجوم الخالد، وجميع الموقرين الآخرين."

"امتلاك جزء من السماء والأرض، هذا ما يستلزمه لورد الداو!"

من الناحية النظرية، يمكن لفانغ يوان أن يزرع جميع المسارات ويصبح موقرًا لجميع المسارات!
